Storage pod questions

?How do Contain-It PODS work?

We deliver a storage unit to your property and our technicians assemble it in the location of your choice. Keep your belongings in it as long as you need; when you are done, we disassemble and collect the empty unit.

?How big is the unit?

The unit is 2.1m by 3m (68 sq ft of floor space), 2 metres tall, and weighs roughly 370kg. It sits on adjustable self-levelling feet, set up by our technicians.

?How is it charged?

Hire is charged weekly, with payment collected every four weeks. At booking we take the installation fee and the first four weeks of hire, which is the minimum hire period. There is no maximum.

?Can I assemble it myself and save the installation fee?

No. The unit is transported and assembled only by our trained technicians, so the setup is correct and safe.

?What about security and insurance?

The door carries two deadlocks; engage both whenever the unit is unattended. You are responsible for securing and insuring the unit and its contents. Stored contents are often covered by standard home contents policies, so check with your insurer. Contain-It is not liable for loss or damage to the contents.

?What areas do you deliver to?

Greater Glasgow, Lanarkshire, Paisley, Falkirk and Ayrshire. If your postcode is outside those areas, get in touch and we will see what we can do.
