Self storage in Glasgow and Paisley

Two family-run yards, four sizes of storage unit, and no fixed contract at either of them.

Self storage in Glasgow

Our Glasgow yard is on Jessie Street in Polmadie, two minutes from Junction 1 of the M74 and five minutes from the city centre. That puts us on the doorstep of Govanhill, the Gorbals, Shawlands, Toryglen and Rutherglen, and an easy motorway run from anywhere along the M74 or M8.

Drive your car, van or trailer right up to the container door and load straight in. No lifts, no corridors, no trolleys, and no booking a time slot: your gate code works any hour of any day, and every entry is logged.

Storage container hire works out considerably cheaper than conventional indoor self storage because you are not paying for heated corridors and lifts you never use. If what you are storing is furniture, boxes, stock or equipment, that saving goes straight in your pocket.

Self storage in Paisley

The Paisley yard sits on Fulbar Road on the west side of town, behind the Aldi, about ten minutes from the town centre. Renfrew, Johnstone, Linwood, Elderslie and Barrhead are all a short drive, and the M8 puts Glasgow Airport five minutes away.

It runs exactly like Glasgow: the same four container sizes, the same 24 hour gate-code access, the same HD CCTV, high fencing and dusk-to-dawn lighting, and the same monthly rolling terms. For anyone in Renfrewshire it saves the trek into the city for storage.

Both yards suit house moves, flat clearances, tradespeople who want tools out of the van overnight, and businesses holding stock. If you are not sure which size you need, call and we will talk it through.

How storing with us works

1. Pick a size. Four storage units from 37 to 150 sq ft, priced per month plus VAT. Tell us what you are storing and we will point you at the right one.

2. Get your gate code. A deposit equivalent to one month's charge is taken at the start, then storage rolls monthly. You get a free heavy-duty padlock on move-in day.

3. Drive in and load. Bring the car, van or trailer right up to your container door, any hour of any day.

4. Leave whenever. No fixed contract, just 30 days' notice, and you can move to a bigger or smaller unit at any point.
