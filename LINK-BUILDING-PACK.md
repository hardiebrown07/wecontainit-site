# Contain-It link building pack

Everything here is drafted ready to send, but nothing goes out until you say so.
Golden rule: never buy links, never reply to the SEO spam in the info@ inbox.
Target pace: 1-2 new real links a month is genuinely enough to change the picture in a year.

## 1. Press pitch: the Paisley story (strongest link available to us)

Send to: news@paisleydailyexpress.co.uk and the Glasgow Times business desk (newsdesk@glasgowtimes.co.uk)
Subject: New self storage yard opens in Paisley, family firm expands from Glasgow

> Hi,
>
> We are a Glasgow family business, Contain-It, and we have just opened our second
> container self storage site on Fulbar Road in Paisley.
>
> The business started at our Polmadie yard off the M74 and has grown to the point
> where we needed a second location. The Paisley site offers drive-up storage
> containers with 24/7 access for households and local tradespeople.
>
> Happy to give a quote, photos of the new yard, or a quick chat if this is of
> interest for the business pages. More at https://wecontainit.co.uk/paisley
>
> Thanks,
> [name], Contain-It, 0141 423 0544

## 4. Memberships that come with a link

- Glasgow Chamber of Commerce, member directory listing
- Renfrewshire Chamber of Commerce, same for the Paisley site
- Self Storage Association UK, industry body, listing carries weight with customers too
These cost membership fees, so they are your call on value; the links are a side benefit.
