About the storage

Your own lockable steel container with your number on the door. Drive your car, van or trailer right up to it, load straight in, and come back any hour of any day with your personal gate code.

It rolls monthly with no fixed contract. A deposit equivalent to one month's charge is taken at the start. Give 30 days' notice when you're done, or swap to a bigger or smaller unit whenever your needs change.

What's part of the service

  • 24/7 access with your own gate code
  • Free heavy-duty padlock on move-in day
  • HD CCTV, fencing and dusk-to-dawn lighting
  • Staff on site to help you move in and out

Storage tips

  • Heavy at the bottom. Load appliances and boxes of books first, light and fragile things on top.
  • Leave a walkway. Keep a narrow path down the middle so you can reach things at the back.
  • Ask about shelving. We can fit shelves in your container so nothing has to sit in a pile.
