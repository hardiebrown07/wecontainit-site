Storage questions, answered

Prices, unit sizes, access, security and contracts at our Glasgow and Paisley locations.

?How much does a storage unit cost in Glasgow?

Our storage units start at £100 a month plus VAT for a 5ft container (37 sq ft), rising to £150 a month for a 20ft container (150 sq ft). Container storage works out considerably cheaper than conventional indoor self storage, and the longer you store the better the price.

?What size storage unit do I need?

As a rough guide: a 5ft container holds the contents of a small shed, a 7ft holds a small bedroom, a 10ft holds a one-bedroom house, and a 20ft holds a two or three-bedroom house. If you are between sizes, go larger so you can walk in and reach things at the back.

?What's the minimum stay?

A calendar month. After that it rolls monthly, and you give 30 days' notice when you want to leave. You can move to a bigger or smaller container at any point.

?When can I get to my things?

Any hour, any day of the year. Your gate code opens the entry gate and your key opens your door. You never need to book a visit.

?How do payments work?

A deposit equivalent to one month's charge is taken at the start, on top of your first month. We'll go through payment options with you when you book. Store longer and the price gets better.

?Do I need insurance for my storage unit?

You are responsible for insuring the contents of your unit. Many home contents policies extend to goods in storage, so it is worth checking with your insurer before you take out anything separate.

?Can I drive up to my storage unit?

Yes. You bring your car, van or trailer right up to your container door and load straight in. There are no lifts, corridors or trolleys to deal with.

?How secure are the locations?

HD CCTV and infra-red cameras record around the clock. Sliding entry gates open with customer-specific codes and every entry is logged. High fencing and dusk-to-dawn lighting cover both locations.

?Do you supply padlocks?

Yes. You get a free heavy-duty padlock on move-in day. We can also fit shelving in your container on request.

?Can you take deliveries for me?

Yes. We accept and sign for deliveries in office hours, keep them in our holding area or put them straight into your container, and let you know they've arrived.

?Can I store a car or motorbike?

A 20ft container will take a car, and smaller units suit motorbikes and quads. Get in touch and we'll talk through which size suits the vehicle.

?Can businesses use the storage?

Yes. Plenty of our storers are tradespeople and small businesses keeping stock, tools, equipment and archive files. The same monthly rolling terms apply.

?What can't I store?

Anything dangerous: fireworks, chemicals, fuel and the like. If you're not sure about something, ask us first.

?Where are you based?

We have two locations: 100 Jessie Street, Polmadie, Glasgow G42 0PG, just off Junction 1 of the M74, and 4 Fulbar Road, Paisley PA2 9AP, ten minutes from the town centre.
