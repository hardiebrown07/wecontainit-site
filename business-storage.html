Made for tradespeople and small businesses

Plenty of our storers are joiners, electricians, landscapers, online sellers and shops. A container gives you a lockable steel store two minutes from the van: keep tools and materials out of the house, hold stock close to your customers, or archive paperwork without paying office rates.

Load in seconds, not trips
